## Why the council landed here
- B fails purchasing power. Retail spends ₹0 to ₹700 a month across five incumbents, and ₹299 at 10%+ churn cannot pay for acquiring the customer (Hormozi, Starving Crowd S002 and LTV:CAC S070).
- The founder's lean toward B was made up for imagined users, not noticed from real ones (Graham, S098).
- RAs are a narrow, deep well. They have a budget and a compliance-shaped need, and 7 to 10 firms reach ₹1 lakh MRR (Graham, Hormozi).
- D carries no ruin risk and acts as a credibility asset for the RA pitch (Buffett S095, Gary V S143).
