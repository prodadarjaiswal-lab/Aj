# Brief: buy Nvidia (NVDA) now or not

**Decision:** whether an individual investor should buy NVDA stock now, after a six-week fall. Date: 13 Nov 2026.

**The facts (FMP data and news reports, 13 Nov 2026)**
- Price $160.76 (down 3.3% today). 52-week range $148.80 to $243.37. Down 30% in one month, 14% year to date. Market cap $3.87 trillion. 50-day average $216, 200-day $204.
- Why it fell: (1) from late October, AI chip stocks sold off on fears of a 2027 data-centre spending slowdown, rising bond yields and stress in the debt that funds "neocloud" customers; NVDA fell 9.3% on 5 Nov alone. (2) On 12 Nov Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and director, effective immediately (company 8-K). Press reports tie it to extramarital affairs, one with a woman US officials reportedly believe is a Chinese spy; an FBI and company investigation is reported, and Congress may call him. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO; the board is searching inside and outside. (3) On 11-12 Nov at least nine brokers cut targets: Evercore $465 to $186 (downgrade), Baird $500 to $200 (downgrade, citing a possible 2027 slowdown), Bernstein $400 to $200 (downgrade), Truist to $174 (downgrade), Cantor to $250 and Morgan Stanley $300 (both still Overweight).
- The business: last quarter (to 26 Jul 2026) revenue $96.2bn, up 106% on the year; net income $59.7bn; gross margin 75%. Trailing 12-month EPS $7.97, so trailing P/E about 20. Free cash flow per share $5.25 (yield 3.3%). Debt is small against cash flow, but debt due in 1 to 5 years rose to $15bn and Nvidia is reported to be helping arrange about $500bn of customer financing with private equity firms and banks.
- Estimates (likely to be cut): EPS $9.29 for the year to Jan 2027 (17x), $15.71 for the year to Jan 2028 (10x). Nvidia's CFO said in August that top-five cloud capex would be near $800bn in 2026 and $1.3tn in 2027.
- Next event: Q3 results on 17 Nov 2026, four days away (consensus revenue about $104-109bn, EPS about $2.4), the first call without Huang.
- Chair's scenario maths (Jan 2028 year): bear EPS $9 x 15 = $135; base EPS $12 x 18 = $216; bull EPS $15.7 x 20 = $314.

**Options**
- **A. Buy the full intended position now**, before results.
- **B. Buy in parts:** about one third now, the rest after the 17 Nov results and once the CEO and investigation picture is clearer.
- **C. Wait:** buy nothing until after results and a named permanent CEO, then decide.
- **D. Don't buy** NVDA at all.

**The investor (assumed):** an India-based individual who trades from a phone, would buy through an overseas account in dollars, no existing NVDA holding, horizon 1 to 3 years, position no more than 5 to 10% of savings, no borrowed money.

**Success (assumed):** beat a Nasdaq index fund over 2 years without a permanent loss above 30% on the position.

**Lean (assumed):** "It has fallen 30%, the business is still growing 100%, so it must be cheap." The council should test that lean.
