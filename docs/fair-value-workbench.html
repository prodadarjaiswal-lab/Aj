Checks before you trust the number

    Nvidia on 13 Nov 2026: wait, do not buy yet

    • The blend is below the price, so the stock is not cheap even after falling 30% in a month. The scenario method is the only one above the price.
    • Free cash flow is two thirds of reported profit. Customers owe Nvidia $63bn and that rose $22bn in one quarter, which fits the reports that it helps finance its buyers.
    • Results on 17 Nov are the first without Jensen Huang, who resigned on 12 Nov. A four-seat Roast Room council scored waiting for those results 6.8 out of 10 and buying a part now 5.3.
    • After the results, buy a first third only if revenue is at least $104bn, estimates for the year to January 2028 hold near $11 to $12 a share, and the price is about $150 or less. At about $115 or less the 20% discount is there.

    Inputs: FMP statements to 26 Jul 2026 and the quote on 13 Nov 2026. Scenario EPS and chances are judgment, set after analyst cuts on 11 and 12 Nov. This is analysis, not personal investment advice.
