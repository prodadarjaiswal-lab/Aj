# Rakesh Steels: Dhanteras & Diwali greeting, a treatment

**Style:** Art Deco (gold lines on warm black, everything around one centre axis, lights that switch on in counted order).
**Format:** 1080×1350 (4:5, the poster's shape), 24 fps, 32 s. Music and sound effects, no voice. All text is drawn into the picture (no subtitles needed; no `.srt`).
**Source:** the client's Dhanteras poster (`ref/poster.png`): steel bar pyramid with a diya on top, steel discs and rings, marigolds, a saw throwing a stream of sparks, a lotus ornament, the shloka, the blessing, RAKESH STEELS · Howrah · 8013626232. Added on request: diyas lighting, rangoli, fireworks, sparkles, lotus, and a Diwali greeting.

## 1. Three structures

1. **The spark's journey** (a single journey). A saw cuts a steel bar; one spark breaks free, travels to the unlit diya on top of the bar pyramid and lights it; that one flame spreads into a festival of light that ends as the poster.
2. **The machine kaleidoscope** (a pattern that locks). Steel discs, rings and bar sections slide in from the edges, mirror and rotate, and lock into a rangoli with a diya in the middle. Pretty, but it has no turn: nothing changes, it only assembles.
3. **The countdown dial** (the number is the plot). A deco dial counts the days from Dhanteras to Diwali. Clear, but cold; it turns a greeting into a calendar.

**Chosen: 1, with 2 as its peak.** The client's own image already tells this story (the saw's sparks flowing into the diya flame), so the film plays it out: the steel works' spark becomes the festival's light. The kaleidoscope rangoli, made from the firm's own products, becomes the signature shot inside the journey.

Opening image: the cut face of a round steel bar filling the frame, a saw coming down its centre line (not a logo, not a slow fade-in). Ending: the film flattens into the poster, with the brand name lit by one last spark, rhyming with the first.

## 2. Logline and arc

*A single spark from a saw cutting steel finds its way to an unlit diya, and its flame lights up the whole night for Rakesh Steels' Dhanteras and Diwali.*

- **Setup (0–8.5 s):** the workshop. Hard metal, a cut, sparks. One spark, the only saturated colour in the picture (saffron), drifts down along a gold line.
- **Turn (8.5 s):** after a held breath of near-silence, the spark lands on the diya and it lights. Warm light floods the steel.
- **Ending (8.5–31 s):** the light is counted out (diyas light in pairs along a deco arch), steel becomes rangoli, the sky answers with fireworks, and everything settles into the greeting card with the brand.

## 3. Benchmark

- **The Great Gatsby (2013) title sequence**: learn gold line drawing itself on black and the sheen sweep on a hit. Don't take its shapes, type or music.
- **Busby Berkeley overhead numbers**: learn objects becoming a rotating mirrored pattern that locks to a symbol. Don't take any formation.
- **Indian festive-season brand films** (jewellers', sweet makers'): learn how warmth and one small human light carry the greeting, and how the brand arrives last, never first. Don't take any shot, jingle or slogan.

## 5. Beat sheet

- 0.0 Black with a faint warm vignette, the bar face already visible; saw whine rising.
- 0.5 The saw bites: sunburst of sparks, camera shake, grinding scream.
- 0.5–3.6 The saw cuts down the axis; spark fans pulse; slow push-in.
- 3.6 The saw lifts out; one saffron spark is left hovering at the cut.
- 4.0–8.0 The spark drifts down in an S-curve, drawing a gold trail; the camera pulls back to the pyramid; harp glissando under it.
- 8.0–8.5 **Silence 1**: the spark hangs above the wick; only its sizzle.
- 8.5 **Ignition**: flame, bell, drone, string swell; light sweeps the bars.
- 10.0–15.0 The arch appears; 8 pairs of diyas light, one pair per beat (10.375 → 14.75); title draws on at 10.4, the greeting line at 11.1.
- 14.975–15.7 Lattice doors close on the centre seam (15.375, on the beat) and open overhead.
- 15.7–20.375 Steel pieces fly in on the beats, mirror, rotate; the shloka at 16.2 (line 1) and 16.75 (line 2).
- 20.375 **Lock** (on the beat): the rotation stops, a ring of light runs round it; hold to 21.0.
- 21.0 Match cut: the rangoli ring → first firework burst.
- 21.0–25.2 Rockets in symmetric pairs, bursts on the beat, accelerating; blessing at 21.0.
- 25.2–25.6 **Silence 2**: the last burst's sparks fall; one bell tail.
- 25.6–27.0 The sparks become the card's gold rules and lotus; greeting text rises.
- 27.0–27.6 RAKESH STEELS title bar unfolds from the centre; 27.6 sheen sweep with a spark.
- 27.6–32.0 Hold on the finished card (≥ 4 s); diya flame breathing; final chord rings out.

## 6. Cue map

- **0–8.5, free time.** Saw and metal foley lead. Tanpura-like drone on D fades in from 1.5 s. Harp glissando rising 4.6 → 8.0 under the spark. Near-silence 8.0–8.5.
- **8.5–31, 96 bpm** (beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s, grid starts at 8.5).
  - 8.5 (bar 1): tubular bell D + hand chimes + strings D major swell + drone.
  - 10.0–15.0 (bars 1.6–3.6): tabla theka enters at 10.0; one glockenspiel/chime note per diya pair on beats 10.375 + n·0.625 (n = 0…7), climbing the pentatonic D E F♯ A B D′ E′ F♯′.
  - 15.375: door closes on a timpani hit, then opens with a harp sweep.
  - 16.0–20.375: plucked melody (sitar-like Karplus–Strong with slides) states the theme; flute answers; violins pizzicato; tabla.
  - 20.375 (beat 19): lock: crash + timpani + bell + string stab.
  - 21.0–25.2: fanfare-like brass (trumpet staccato, horns) over tabla doubling its pattern (acceleration: eighths → sixteenths from 23.8); burst hits on the beats.
  - 25.2–25.6: silence except a bell tail.
  - 25.6–32.0: final cadence: strings + flute + harp; tubular bell at 27.6 with a chime glissando for the sheen; ring out to the end.

## 8. Titles and type

- Devanagari has no deco face in the style's fonts, so: **Rozha One** (high-contrast Devanagari display, OFL) for "शुभ धनतेरस"; **Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit** (OFL) for the shloka and the Hindi lines. Latin: **Limelight** for RAKESH STEELS, **Josefin Sans SemiBold** for "Howrah · 8013626232" and "Happy Dhanteras & Diwali", **Poiret One** for small ornaments.
- All gold: banded metal gradient, engraved underline, hairline highlight; titles sit in stepped title bars that unfold from the centre and then get a sheen sweep. The one saturated accent is saffron fire (spark, flames, fireworks' cores); marigolds use a muted deco amber so they don't compete.
- The shloka is written correctly: "शुभं करोति कल्याणम् आरोग्यं धनसम्पदा । दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तुते ॥" (the poster's "कल्याणमू" was a typo for कल्याणम्).
- Every text block holds at least 4 s once drawn on.
